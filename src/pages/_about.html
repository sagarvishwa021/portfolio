05 — About

It started in a notebook nobody read.

Long before I was writing articles or B2B cold emails, I used to write short pieces in my notebooks. They were never meant to be published, and no one else ever read them, they were just written for the sake of writing.

It was the art of it that I actually fell in love with.

A fictional story, a broken poem, or even a strictly structured format like a ghazal, I experimented with them all. And it all started back when I was in the 7th standard.

For a school activity, I was assigned to write a news report on the latest updates in my town. I still remember going to the local vendors, asking what was new in the city, conducting surveys, and noting down our conversations.

I didn’t win the competition, but it made me fall in love with writing. I’ve been experimenting with different styles ever since.

But the real turning point was when I took History as my major in college. History is all about stories. It’s the process of gathering data from archaeological and literary findings and turning them into a structured narrative.

I later applied for my first role as an email copywriter at a recruitment firm and got selected. Working professionally over the last two years has taught me so much about the craft.

Today, when I look back at that little boy writing broken pieces in his notebook, to now writing professionally for audiences, I owe it all to discipline, hunger, and a pure love for writing.
