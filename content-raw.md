About the Project
[IMAGE]
A specialized talent acquisition and recruitment firm was looking for an email copywriter for a cold email prospecting lead sequence.
As the email copywriter on the project, I worked with the client's appointed team to drive clicks, opens and conversions as cold leads progressed through the email marketing lead sequence. The primary challenge was cutting through crowded B2B inboxes, overcoming low response rates from busy hiring managers, and striking the right balance between professional authority and human approachability.
Email Marketing Strategy
[IMAGE]
As the email marketing funnel was specifically created to convert cold leads, it was essential to avoid sounding like an automated sales blast.
The emails would be sent in plain-text emails (rather than heavily styled HTML templates) to ensure deliverability directly into the primary inbox rather than the promotions tab. Instead of pitching generic "recruitment services," emails focused directly on immediate client headaches such as lengthy hiring cycles, talent drop-offs, and under-qualified applicant pools.
Copywriting Strategy
To build trust without being aggressive, I structured cold sequences around a 3-part framework:
- The Opener: A punchy, low-friction hook highlighting a specific industry hiring challenge and introducing a pre-vetted candidate profile or solution.
- The Social Proof Follow-Up: A short case study or metric proving how Reqx solved the exact hiring problem for a similar client.
- The Soft CTA: A zero-pressure closing line asking for interest rather than demanding a 30-minute sales call.
